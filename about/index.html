About

Trailmarker is a project-focused software practice established in 2018. Since then it has built bespoke spatial applications, services and tools for science and enterprise, while contributing regularly to architecture and strategy programs.

Since 2026, Trailmarker has also turned to research on the critical and ethical adoption of AI agents and systems of agents in everyday life.

The term Trailmarker uses for its approach: regulatory pragmatism.

The work underway addresses very broad questions such as:

How can a software agent, or a group of agents, make a consequential decision?

How can we record when a software agent has made a decision?

How can we regiment and interpret the behaviour of software agents we put to use at home and at work, and establish trust and safety?

What does the use of software agents newly demand from its users in terms of accountability and conduct?

How can we work together?

These are only a few of the open questions facing societies in transformation. To learn how Trailmarker is beginning to answer them, get in contact.
